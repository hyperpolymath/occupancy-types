== Reclamation at a cut (Reclaim.agda)

[cols="1,3,1",options="header"]
|===
| ID | Claim | Status
| R-1 | Cutting a linear protocol anywhere factors its grade, in any resource algebra (`Timeline.cut-factor`) | PROVEN
| R-2 | *Reclamation law*: the suffix after a cut starts from the prefix's drift, not its peak; `peak − drift` is reclaimed at the frontier (`reclaim-law`, `occupancy-at-cut`) | PROVEN
| R-3 | *Occupancy ≤ cost*: `peak (xs ++ ys) ≤ peak xs + peak ys` | PROVEN (`occupancy≤cost`, `protocol-occupancy≤cost`)
| R-4 | Equality iff nothing is reclaimed: with drift = peak the occupancy is the cost (`no-reclaim`); with drift < peak and a non-trivial suffix it is strictly less (`reclaimed`) | PROVEN
| R-5 | Two readings of one protocol: occupancy 4, cost 7 (`Witness`) | PROVEN
| R-6 | Reading occupancy as cost, or drift as peak, is refused (`CostIsOccupancy`, `DriftIsPeak`) | TESTED (`tests/reject/`: each is refused at its invalid declaration by `scripts/check-agda.sh`)
|===

== Separation from siblings (criterion 2 of the family bar)

Tropical types cannot state any line of Reclaim.agda: in a cost algebra the
grade of a sequence is the sum, so there is no slack to reclaim. The
separation is computed (R-5, and resource-algebra S-1) and proved in
general (R-3/R-4). Together with the order-sensitivity of independent
threads (choreographic-types A-3), occupancy has two theorems of its own.
